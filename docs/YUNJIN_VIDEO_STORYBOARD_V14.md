# V15 Video-Based Weaving Storyboard / 视频分镜拆解

This is a teaching storyboard distilled from the user's reference direction. It is not a frame-by-frame copy of any video and does not claim craft verification.

## Continuous Action Beats

| Beat | Time window | Loom focus | Draw worker | Lower weaver | Visual purpose |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.00-0.18 | Loom held in ready state | Grounded, small breathing motion | Seated close to cloth, hands near work area | Hold long enough to read the upper/lower relationship. |
| 2 | 0.16-0.46 | Red/gold cord curtain and warp groups lift | Leans forward and pulls down with both arms | Waits, eyes/body oriented toward cloth | Make upper draw action clearly drive the shed, then hold it. |
| 3 | 0.42-0.70 | Shed stays open; shuttle path lights up | Holds the open shed | Reaches into the cloth area and sends shuttle/weft across | Make hand, shuttle and weft read as one connected action. |
| 4 | 0.62-0.86 | Reed/beater moves toward woven edge | Holds position | Body follows the beater, then compresses the pick | Show beating as an actual mechanical settling action. |
| 5 | 0.82-1.00 | Warp returns and cloth roller advances | Releases and resets | Finishes the pick while cloth advances | Make the continuous red/gold cloth visibly grow. |

## V14 Realism Targets

- Replace the purely procedural body silhouette with a licensed CC0 rigged human GLB at scene-scale proportions.
- Keep procedural clothing overlays so the neutral CC0 mannequin reads as a working craft figure.
- Use larger but continuous body motion: pulling, leaning, reaching, treadle press and beater follow-through.
- Make the loom less like a generic wooden frame by emphasizing a red/gold jacquard control wall, dense cord curtain, red cloth bed and gold nodes.
- Keep all historical and mechanical claims conservative: this remains a simulated teaching reconstruction.

## Asset Note

The V15 body base is `assets/models/human-base-rigged.cc0.glb`, downloaded from Innerscene's CC0 Free Parts Library. It provides a 53-bone rigged human body without animations. The weaving actions are authored locally in the Three.js scene.
